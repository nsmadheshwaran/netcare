## 1. What is being sold

## 2. Price and payment
2.1 One-time price: **Rs 14,999** [plus GST at the applicable rate, if the Seller is GST-registered], payable
[in full on signing / in instalments: ___].
2.2 Included in the price: installation, a handover session of up to [2] hours, and [3] months of support under
clause 4. Anything else (extra training, custom changes, travel) is charged at Rs [rate] and agreed in writing
first.
2.3 [Late payment: the Seller may suspend support until overdue amounts are paid.]

## 3. What NetCare does and does not do
3.1 NetCare helps manage sales, stock, service jobs, payments and monitoring. **It is not accounting or
GST-filing software.** Its GST figures and reports are drafts that the Customer's accountant must check.
3.2 NetCare does **not** create GST e-invoices (IRN) or e-way bills.
3.3 Email, SMS and WhatsApp alerts work only if the Customer sets up its own email or messaging provider, and
those providers charge their own fees.
3.4 The Customer is responsible for entering correct business details, tax rates and HSN/SAC codes, and for
having its accountant confirm them before issuing real invoices.

## 4. Support and fixes
4.1 For **3 months** from installation the Seller will fix errors in NetCare that stop it working as described
in its user guide, at no extra charge.
4.2 Support is by [email/phone/WhatsApp] at [contact], on [days and hours]. The Seller aims to reply within
[number] working hours; this is a target, not a guarantee.
4.3 Not covered: problems caused by the Customer's computer, network or other software, changes made by the
Customer or anyone else to the software, loss of data from not keeping backups, and requests for new features.
4.4 After the support period, support is available at **Rs 4,999 per year** (covering fixes and updates under
this clause), or Rs [rate] per hour for one-off help without taking the annual plan.
4.5 New versions: [the Seller will / will not] provide updates after the support period. Updates may be charged.

## 5. Backups and the Customer's responsibilities
5.1 NetCare saves a daily backup on the same computer. **The Customer must copy backups to another disk or a
cloud drive at least [weekly], keep the `.env` file safe, and test a restore when the Seller shows how.**
5.2 The Customer is responsible for who can use its computer and NetCare, for choosing strong passwords, for
keeping Windows and Docker Desktop updated, and for not exposing NetCare to the internet unless the Seller has
agreed a secure setup in writing.
5.3 The Seller is not responsible for data lost because backups were not kept or tested.

## 6. Data and privacy
6.1 All business data entered into NetCare belongs to the Customer and stays on the Customer's own computer.
The Seller does not receive or store it unless the Customer sends it for support, and then only uses it to
give that support.
6.2 The Customer is responsible for the personal data of its own customers and staff stored in NetCare,
including any duties under Indian data-protection law (for example the Digital Personal Data Protection Act,
2023, to the extent it applies). [Your lawyer should check this clause.]

## 7. Warranty and liability
7.1 The Seller will make reasonable efforts to supply software that works as described. **Apart from clause 4,
NetCare is supplied "as is", and the Seller gives no other promise that it is free of errors or suitable for a
particular purpose.**
7.2 The Seller is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit or business, or loss of data.
7.3 The Seller's total liability under this agreement is limited to the price the Customer actually paid.
7.4 Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited. [Your lawyer should check 7.2 and 7.3.]

## 8. Confidentiality
Each side keeps the other's confidential information (including, for the Customer, its business data, and for
the Seller, any source code) private and uses it only for this agreement.
